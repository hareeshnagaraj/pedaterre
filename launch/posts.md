# pedaterre — launch posts

Every figure below traces to `data/generated/index/city-stats.json` or a
named block payload (FY2027 final roll, extract 9d35a870). Nothing invented.

**Register:** dry, specific, no exclamation marks, no emoji, no
engagement-bait. Humour only as an observation about a real number.

**Positioning: apolitical.** These are drawings of the filed record. No
copy names a private owner as a target, argues who should pay, or tags into
a tax-policy thread. The absurd and the spatial carry this; the argument
does not need our help.
